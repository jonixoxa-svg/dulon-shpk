This privacy policy applies to the mobile game **Orbit Dash** (the "App"),
published by **[YOUR NAME OR COMPANY]** ("we", "us"). It explains what
information is processed when you use the App.

## Summary

- Orbit Dash does **not** require an account and does **not** collect your
  name, email address, contacts, photos, files, or precise location.
- Your game progress (high score and settings) is stored **only on your
  device** and is never uploaded anywhere.
- The App shows advertising provided by **Google AdMob**. The AdMob SDK may
  collect certain data automatically to provide and measure ads — see below.

## Data stored on your device

The App saves your high score, sound preference and play counters locally
on your device using the standard Android app storage. This data never
leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. As part of serving
ads, Google may automatically collect and process:

- **Advertising ID** (a resettable device identifier used for advertising)
- **IP address** (used for coarse, city-level geolocation and fraud
  prevention)
- **Device information** (such as device model, operating system version,
  screen size, mobile carrier and language)
- **Ad interaction data** (such as which ads were shown, viewed or tapped,
  and general app usage diagnostics related to ad serving)

This data is collected and processed by Google under Google's own privacy
policy. We do not receive or store any of this information ourselves.

Learn more:

- How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
  <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Types of data AdMob collects:
  <https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9755590>

## Your choices and consent

**European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland:** when you first launch the
App you will be shown a consent dialog (Google's User Messaging Platform)
where you can choose whether ads may use your data for personalization.
You can change your choice at any time from the **privacy settings button
on the App's main menu**.

**Everyone:** you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of
ad personalization, in your device settings under
**Settings → Google → Ads** (or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## Children

Orbit Dash is a general-audience game with no age-restricted content. We
do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are
served through Google AdMob in accordance with Google's ad content
policies.

## Data security and retention

We do not operate any servers for the App and do not store any user data
ourselves. Ad-related data is handled and retained by Google according to
Google's policies linked above.
